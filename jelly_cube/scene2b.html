Scene 02b · What Is Stress?
Normal Stress vs Shear Stress
$\sigma_{nn}$ — Normal Stress
Force perpendicular to the cut face
Compression ($\sigma < 0$)
Pushes inward — molecules resist being crushed
Tension ($\sigma > 0$)
Pulls outward — molecules resist being torn apart
Normal stress always acts $\perp$ to the surface
$\tau_{xy}$ — Shear Stress
Force parallel to the cut face
Shear ($\tau \neq 0$)
Tries to slide the two halves past each other
Shear stress always acts $\parallel$ to the surface
Combined State: $\sigma + \tau$
Both act simultaneously on every cut face
$\mathbf{t} = \sigma_{nn}\hat{n} + \tau\hat{s}$
The traction vector is tilted because of shear!
$\begin{bmatrix} t_x \\ t_y \\ t_z \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \sigma_{xx} & \color{#69db7c}{\tau_{xy}} & \color{#69db7c}{\tau_{xz}} \\ \color{#69db7c}{\tau_{yx}} & \sigma_{yy} & \color{#69db7c}{\tau_{yz}} \\ \color{#69db7c}{\tau_{zx}} & \color{#69db7c}{\tau_{zy}} & \sigma_{zz} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} n_x \\ n_y \\ n_z \end{bmatrix}$
The off-diagonal terms cause the tilt
